PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 2026 • Strict GDPR & Privacy Compliance

1. Commitment to Privacy

This privacy policy outlines how personal information and usage data are handled on the official portfolio website of Prathamesh Fatangade (located at fatangadeprathamesh-svg.github.io/portfolio/). Your privacy and data confidentiality are respected unconditionally. I do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker any visitor data to third parties.

2. Information You Provide Voluntarily

When you initiate contact using the interactive contact form on this website, you voluntarily provide:

  • Full Name: To address you properly in professional correspondence.
  • Email Address: To reply directly to your technical, academic, or collaboration inquiry.
  • Message Content: The inquiry or message text you author.

This information is transmitted directly to fatangadeprathamesh@gmail.com for communication purposes only. No external marketing databases or mailing lists are populated.

3. Analytics & Cookies

This website respects privacy-first standards:

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4): Aggregated, anonymous metrics (e.g. general geographic region, page navigation, device category) may be collected to gauge interest in published research and open-source projects only when you explicitly grant consent through the cookie banner.
  • Client-Side Storage: Your visual theme preference (Wine Red Dark vs Light Sand) and cookie consent selection are stored locally in your browser's localStorage so your preference persists across reloads. No tracking identifiers are stored.

4. Spam Protection & Integrity

To defend against automated bot exploitation, the contact form includes client-side honeypot verification and rate-limiting cooldown timers. These mechanisms verify human interaction without harvesting behavioral fingerprints.

5. Your Rights & Inquiries

You have full rights to request the inspection or immediate deletion of any past email communications. For questions regarding this privacy policy or your information, reach out directly at fatangadeprathamesh@gmail.com.
