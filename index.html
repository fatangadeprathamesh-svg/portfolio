Research Work & Publications

Peer-reviewed international journal publication & extended book chapter

IJIRT International Journal Impact Factor: 8.412 ISSN: 2349-6002 Volume 13, Issue 5 • October 2026 Registration ID: 209192

SMART AGRICULTURE ROBOT FOR CROP MONITORING USING IOT SENSING, AUTONOMOUS NAVIGATION AND EDGE AI

Authors: Prathamesh Fatangade¹, Tejas Kalaskar², Yash Jagdale³, Yash Hinge⁴, Dr. Sunil Mahajan⁵* (Corresponding Author)

MAEER’s MIT Arts, Commerce and Science College, Alandi (D), Pune, Maharashtra, India

Abstract: Smart agriculture integrates sensing, communication, automation, data analytics, and decision support to improve crop management. This research presents the design and analysis of an autonomous ground-based robot for crop scouting across field rows. Combining environmental sensors, soil capacitive probes, RGB imaging, edge computing, and crop-row navigation, the system performs geo-referenced crop scouting, soil-moisture mapping, microclimate tracking, and uncertainty-aware AI disease screening for proactive farmer decision support.

Mechanical & Power Architecture

4-wheel differential-drive chassis with low center of gravity, LiFePO₄ battery management, and hardware emergency-stop safety.

IoT Multi-Sensor Suite

Capacitive soil moisture sensing, microclimate monitoring (temp/humidity/light), and obstacle avoidance sensor fusion.

Edge AI & Vision Intelligence

On-board convolutional neural network screening for early leaf disease symptoms, canopy vigour, and weed presence with confidence scores.

Decision Support Dashboard

Geo-referenced field mapping, proactive threshold alerts, and cloud telemetry synchronization for actionable farm intervention.
